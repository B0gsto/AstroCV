# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, sourcers, engineering leads and potential B2B clients evaluating Bogdan Stoica for Software Engineer, integration, cloud and AI workflow contractor opportunities. They usually arrive from LinkedIn or a shared link, scan quickly, and need to understand seniority, technical direction, credibility and fit without reading a full traditional CV.

## Product Purpose

This site presents Bogdan as a software engineer who can own practical systems across cloud infrastructure, product development, integrations and AI workflow tooling. Success means the visitor leaves with a clear impression: he is precise, current, technically serious and worth contacting for software engineering work.

## Brand Personality

Clean, precise, technically confident. The tone should feel calm and competent, with enough visual craft to feel memorable, but without decorative noise. It should impose respect through restraint, clarity and evidence of real engineering judgment.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site look vibe-coded, like gradient spam, glassmorphism by default, generic SaaS template, terminal cosplay, or an obviously AI-generated portfolio. Avoid decorative gradients that do not explain hierarchy, repeated identical card grids, hero-metric templates, and visual elements that compete with the content.

## Design Principles

1. Respect through restraint: use fewer visual effects, stronger spacing, cleaner type and deliberate contrast.
2. Evidence over labels: show concrete work, stack, systems and current learning direction instead of relying on broad self-description.
3. Coherent engineering narrative: connect Azure, product engineering, AI workflows, integrations and Rust learning into one believable trajectory.
4. Scan first, depth second: make the first minute useful for recruiters, then provide richer detail for technical readers.
5. Fancy through precision: the design should feel premium because alignment, rhythm, contrast and interaction details are exact, not because it is visually loud.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, readable line lengths, semantic markup, keyboard-friendly navigation, responsive layouts without horizontal overflow, and reduced-motion support. Motion may be used, but it must stay subtle and never gate content visibility.
